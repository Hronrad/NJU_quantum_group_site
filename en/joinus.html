Join the group

We welcome students and researchers interested in quantum information, algorithms and their connections with artificial intelligence.

Who should contact us

  1. Undergraduates
    Students who want to begin with reading, discussion and foundational research training.
  2. Graduate applicants
    Applicants with backgrounds in physics, mathematics, computer science or related disciplines.
  3. Visitors and collaborators
    Researchers interested in academic exchange or collaboration on shared problems.

What to include

Briefly describe your background, research interests and the form of participation you have in mind. Please use the official group email once it is confirmed.
