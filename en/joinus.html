Join the group

We welcome students and researchers interested in quantum foundations, quantum information and communication, algorithms, quantum neural networks and quantum AI.

Who should contact us

  1. Undergraduates
    Students who want to enter quantum information through courses, reading, discussion and foundational research training.
  2. Graduate applicants
    Applicants with backgrounds in physics, mathematics, computer science or related disciplines who wish to study quantum foundations, algorithms, communication or quantum AI.
  3. Visitors and collaborators
    Researchers interested in exchange around quantum theory, quantum-information physics, quantum communication or intelligent information processing.

What to include

Please briefly describe your academic background, research interests, relevant coursework or research experience, and the form of participation you have in mind. Graduate admissions and enrollment follow Nanjing University and School of Physics notices for the relevant year.
