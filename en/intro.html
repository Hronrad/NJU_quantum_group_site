About the group

Overview

The NJU Quantum Information Group studies foundational questions in quantum mechanics, quantum information and algorithms, as well as quantum neural networks and quantum AI. Our work connects rigorous theoretical structures with implementable information-processing tasks.

We turn conceptual questions in quantum mechanics into information problems that can be calculated, compared and tested.

Research areas

QF

Quantum foundations and measurement

Complementarity, uncertainty relations, quantum measurement and characterisations of quantum correlations.

QIA

Quantum algorithms and networks

Quantum operations, quantum walks, sparse coding and networked quantum computation.

QML

Quantum machine learning

Representation, classification and generation with quantum models, including possible quantum advantages.

How we work

We begin with a precise problem definition, then test assumptions using mathematical analysis, numerical simulation and complexity comparison. Reproducible derivations, open discussion and agreement between physical intuition and computational evidence guide the work.
